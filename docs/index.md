# Robot Build Manual

This site is the canonical hardware build and bring-up manual for the RoboRacer robot. It exists to collect repeatable, reproducible build instructions, wiring diagrams, firmware flashing steps, and troubleshooting guidance that are *not* course-specific.

## Who this is for
- Team members who assemble, wire, flash, and bring-up robots.
- New contributors who need a single place to get a robot from kit to running.
- Mentors and maintainers who document lessons learned and approved procedures.

## How this differs from the course site
- This manual documents the long-term, canonical build process, approved parts, and maintenance procedures. It is stable and consumed by the team across semesters.
- The course site contains lecture materials, assignments, and transient course-specific guidance that change every term.
- Use this manual for "how to build and bring-up" the hardware; use the course site for teaching and curriculum.
